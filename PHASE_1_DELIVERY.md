## 1. What We Are Building

Phase 1 delivers a fully live, fully operational Mahrea website — everything a customer needs to discover, browse, and buy jewellery, and everything the Mahrea team needs to run the business without depending on a developer.

This is not a prototype or a demo. Phase 1 is a production-ready launch.

---

## 2. What "Dynamic" Means for Mahrea

Every piece of content on the Mahrea website is connected to the admin panel. This means:

- When you add a new product in the admin → it appears on the website instantly.
- When you change a price → customers see the new price within seconds.
- When you upload a new hero banner → the homepage updates without anyone touching code.
- When you add a testimonial → it shows on the homepage that same day.

**There is no hardcoded content on the Mahrea website.** Everything the customer sees — products, images, prices, banners, categories, testimonials, trust badges — is managed entirely through your admin panel.

## 5. What Is NOT in Phase 1

To ensure a focused, on-time delivery, the following are deliberately excluded:

- No COD — Razorpay UPI and Cards only
- No in-app return management — customers are directed to WhatsApp
- No discount codes or coupons
- No product reviews or star ratings
- No loyalty points
- No abandoned cart recovery emails
- No sales analytics dashboard
- No blog
- No AR try-on
- No multi-language or multi-currency support

These features are planned and will be delivered in subsequent phases.
